About

The unter Behind the Hawke

photo by Eugene Mayo

I listen for the story.

I’ve been creating and experimenting within film and music, my two favorite media, for over ten years. Since moving to NYC, staying active as a sound professional has deepened my understanding of both crafts.

Working up close with other creatives and technicians day in and day out has taught me to adapt to all kinds of working styles, so I can show up on any set and meet every production’s sound needs.

I also love writing, directing and producing when the right project comes along. In the hustle of it all, it’s important to keep our own creative worlds alive!
